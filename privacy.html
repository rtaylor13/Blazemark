Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

1. Introduction

Blazemark LLC ("Blazemark," "we," "us," or "our") is an AI implementation and consulting firm based in Corpus Christi and Austin, Texas. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect personal information when you visit https://blazemark.co (the "Site"), contact us, or otherwise interact with us online.

This policy covers the Site and our pre-engagement communications. Personal information we process on a client's behalf during a paid engagement is governed by the written agreement with that client, which controls if it conflicts with this policy.

By using the Site, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Site.

2. Information We Collect

Information you give us. When you submit our contact form or email us, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your company name
  • The service you are interested in
  • The contents of your message and any other details you choose to share

If you become a client, we may also collect business contact details, billing information, and project-related information as described in your service agreement.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider and the services that deliver the Site may automatically log technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, pages viewed, and the date and time of your visit.

Information from others. We may receive information about you from referral partners or colleagues who introduce you to us, or from publicly available business sources such as company websites and professional networking profiles.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information (such as government ID numbers, financial account numbers, health information, or precise geolocation) through the Site. Please do not send it to us through the contact form.

3. How We Use Information

We use personal information to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and schedule calls to scope potential work
  • Provide, manage, and bill for the services you engage us to perform
  • Send project updates, weekly progress reports, and other service communications
  • Operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Site
  • Detect and prevent spam, fraud, and abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use the information you submit through the Site to train AI models.

If you are a client, we do not use your business data for any purpose other than delivering your engagement, unless your agreement says otherwise.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The Site does not currently use advertising cookies, analytics trackers, or cross-site tracking pixels. Our third-party service providers may use cookies, local storage, or similar technologies that are strictly necessary to deliver the Site securely and to process contact form submissions.

You can set your browser to block or delete cookies. Doing so should not affect your ability to browse the Site.

If we add analytics or other non-essential tracking technologies in the future, we will update this policy and, where required by law, ask for your consent first.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. Because we do not track you across websites or sell or share personal information for targeted advertising, we do not change our practices in response to Do Not Track signals. If we ever begin such activities, we will honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals as an opt-out where required by law.

6. Data Retention and Security

Retention. We keep contact form submissions and related correspondence for as long as needed to respond to you and evaluate a potential engagement, generally no longer than 24 months after our last contact. We keep client records for as long as the engagement lasts and afterward as needed for legal, tax, accounting, and dispute-resolution purposes. When information is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it.

Security. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information. These include encryption in transit (HTTPS), access controls, and limiting access to people who need it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, including Texas (under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act), California, other U.S. states with privacy laws, the European Economic Area, and the United Kingdom, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate personal information
  • Delete your personal information
  • Obtain a copy of your information in a portable format
  • Opt out of the sale of personal information, targeted advertising, or profiling (we do not engage in these activities)
  • Object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

How to make a request. Email us at blazemarkconsulting@gmail.com with the subject line "Privacy Request." We will respond within 45 days, or sooner if the law requires. We may need to verify your identity first. You may use an authorized agent where the law allows.

Appeals. If we decline your request, you may appeal by replying to our decision with the subject line "Privacy Appeal." If you are not satisfied with the result, you may contact your state attorney general or, in the EEA or UK, your local data protection authority.

No discrimination. We will not treat you differently for exercising your privacy rights.

Marketing emails. You can opt out of non-essential emails at any time by using the unsubscribe link or by emailing us.

Legal bases (EEA and UK). We process personal information to take steps at your request before entering a contract, to perform a contract, to pursue our legitimate interests in running and improving our business, to comply with legal obligations, and with your consent where required.
