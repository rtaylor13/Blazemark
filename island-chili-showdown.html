About the Showdown

The Island Chili Showdown is an annual cook-off and community fundraiser held on Padre Island in Corpus Christi, Texas. Teams compete for glory, bragging rights, and the love of great chili — all while raising critical funds for the Texas Sealife Center.

The Texas Sealife Center is a nonprofit dedicated to the rescue, rehabilitation, and release of coastal and aquatic wildlife in South Texas — including endangered sea turtles. Every bowl you taste and every dollar you donate helps more animals make it back to the ocean.

🐢
Saving Sea Turtles

100% of proceeds benefit the Texas Sealife Center's wildlife rescue operations on the Gulf Coast.

Schedule of Events

Here's what the day looks like. Come early, stay late, eat lots of chili.

9:00 AM
Cook Team Check-In & Setup
Teams arrive, pick their spot in the lot, and get their equipment fired up.
10:00 AM
Cooking Begins
Officially, the burners are lit. No pre-made chili allowed — everything starts here.
11:00 AM
Gates Open to the Public
Tasters, supporters, and families are welcome. Donations encouraged at the gate.
12:30 PM
Judging Begins
Official judges make the rounds. Score sheets cover flavor, heat, consistency, and creativity.
2:30 PM
Awards Ceremony
Winners announced! Trophies, bragging rights, and a whole lot of chili glory.
3:00 PM
Event Closes
Thanks for coming. See you next year — same island, more chili.

Register a Cook Team

Entry fee is $25 per team (up to 4 people). All fees go directly to the Texas Sealife Center.

  • Teams may have up to 4 members. Only one entry per team.
  • All chili must be prepared on-site on the day of the event — no pre-cooking.
  • Each team is responsible for their own equipment, fuel, ingredients, and setup.
  • Chili recipes can be any style: Texas Red, verde, white, vegetarian — get creative.
  • A minimum of one quart of chili must be available for judging and public tasting.
  • Teams must keep their area clean and follow all venue safety guidelines.
  • Team names must be family-friendly (this is a community and wildlife event!).
Register via Email Or use PayPal: @islandchilishowdown
